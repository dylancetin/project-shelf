Shelf runs locally in your new tab. No redirect, no loading-page flash, and no autofocus stealing the browser’s address bar.

Local cache

The frontend and bundled icons are installed with the extension. Saved destinations appear immediately while live data refreshes. Server actions are never cached or queued.
